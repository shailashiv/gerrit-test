Commit Message

[FSW-992] fs: rst drv: modify wait for mfc halt

As per JIRA-H15B-2480, we need to wait for idle and
stop bsy bit's are set and then write 0x0 to stop ctrl
to bring down the stop bsy bit.

Once stop busy bit is down, we can confirm mfc's are
halted.
