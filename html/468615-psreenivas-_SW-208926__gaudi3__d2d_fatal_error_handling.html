Commit Message

[SW-208926] gaudi3: d2d fatal error handling

It is observed that even after uncorrectable
d2d fatal errors, the setups recovered after
watchdog reset. This patch adds support to try
and recover the card atleast once after fatal
error by issung hard reset, if the fatal errors
are seen even after this the card will be marked
for rma and booting will be halted.
